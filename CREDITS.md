# Credits

Everything in Cartografo is drawn in code, except the files listed here.

## Photographic textures — `public/textures/`

All from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No attribution is required; we give it anyway.
Files were resized (512 px ground, 256 px roofs) and re-encoded as JPEG; roofs are turned grey at load time
and blended over each building's own colour.

(three.js, light from the upper left), then given an ink outline and a drop shadow, and saved as WebP.
Sets were split into single objects; weapons and the shield are shown lying on the floor.
